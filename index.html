My Rules

Read these before every session.

The 3 hard rules

  • 1. Trend only. No counter-trend. Ever.
  • 2. Max 2 trades a day.
  • 3. Max loss $400 a day. Then I'm done.

Risk

  • Risk $150 max per trade.
  • Minimum 2R to the final target.
  • Stop 75 pts or less. Bigger = skip.
  • Max 4 MNQ contracts.
  • Never move a stop further away.
  • Never add to a loser.
  • After a -$300 day, next day is half size.

Trade window

  • 9:45 to 11:00 AM ET only.
  • Cancel unfilled limits at 11:00. Flat by 11:00 (?)

Setup steps, in order

  • Mark 4H high and low before 9:45.
  • Trend check on 1H at 9:40. Up, down or range.
  • Sweep into an HTF level.
  • 5m BOS (candle body close).
  • Take the first FVG only.
  • Limit entry at 70.5% OTE inside it.
  • Stop 2 pts beyond the sweep.

No-trade list

  • Any checklist item is a NO.
  • Trend is RANGE.
  • Sweep goes against the trend.
  • Distracted, tired or bill pressure.
  • Big news at 10:00: skip 9:55 to 10:10 (?)
  • 4H range too small for 2R.
  • Sweep candle held outside = a real break.
  • Revenge or "make it back" feeling.

Stop for the day

  • Down $400? Close the platform.
  • 2 trades taken? Done.
  • Up $500? Done.
  • 2 rule breaks? Done.
  • No journal entry = a rule break.
  • Second trade after a rule break? Never.
From Rulebook v2.1 (draft). (?) = default to confirm.
